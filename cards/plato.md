## Kort 1 · Årskurs 6 — The student who writes it all into books

**Kategori:** `civilizations`

### Korttext

> Mr X lives in Athens, a city in Greece.  
> His teacher asks people questions at the big square.  
> His teacher does not write books.  
> One day, a big court in the city says his teacher must die.  
> Mr X is very sad and travels to other countries.  
> Later he comes back and opens a school in a quiet garden.  
> The school is called the Academy.  
> A young student named Aristotle studies at his school.  
> Mr X writes many books.  
> His books look like conversations.  
> In his books, his teacher asks questions, just like in real life.  
> Mr X writes, so his teacher's ideas never die.  
> **Who is Mr X?**

### Svarsalternativ

- ✅ Plato
- ⬜ Socrates
- ⬜ Aristotle
- ⬜ Pythagoras
- ⬜ Confucius

### Ordförklaringar (marginal)

- **Academy** — *the name of his school. Many schools use this name today.*
- **conversation** — *two or more people talking together*
- **square** — *an open place in the middle of a town*
- **scroll** — *a long piece of paper that you roll up*

### Huvudbild

- **Motiv:** Plato as a friendly teacher sitting with a small group of teenage and young adult students under olive trees in a sunny grove near Athens, with a simple colonnade among the trees in the distance. This shows the Academy, his garden school. Production note: the Academy is an olive grove outside the city walls, and its students are teenagers and adults, not small children. Do not use photos of the modern 19th-century Academy of Athens building.
- **Bildtext:** *Mr X teaches students in a garden in Athens.*
- **Shutterstock-sökning:** `ancient Greek philosopher teaching students olive grove illustration`
- **AI-prompt (alternativ):** Warm, friendly illustration for children: the ancient Greek philosopher Plato with a beard and simple robe sits on a stone bench in a sunny olive grove, talking with a small group of teenage and young adult students, a simple colonnade with softly painted columns among the trees in the distance, soft colors, no text.

### Sidobild

- **Motiv:** A papyrus scroll with ancient Greek writing and a reed pen, showing how Plato writes his books by hand. Helps explain the word 'scroll' and that his books are written as conversations.
- **Bildtext:** *He writes his books on a scroll.*
- **Shutterstock-sökning:** `ancient papyrus scroll greek writing reed pen`
- **AI-prompt (alternativ):** Simple clear illustration of an open papyrus scroll with a few lines of ancient Greek letters and a reed pen lying next to it, warm parchment colors, plain background, no modern objects, no readable modern text.

## Kort 2 · Årskurs 9 — The cave and the shadows

**Kategori:** `civilizations`

### Korttext

> Mr X lives in Athens about 2,400 years ago.  
> His teacher is Socrates.  
> Mr X writes a famous story about a cave.  
> Prisoners sit in a dark cave.  
> They are chained, so they can only look at the wall.  
> A fire behind them makes shadows on the wall.  
> They think the shadows are the whole world.  
> One prisoner gets free and sees the sun.  
> He goes back to tell the others.  
> They do not believe him.  
> Mr X says: learning is like walking out of the cave.  
> Today, a phone screen can be like that wall.  
> **Who is Mr X?**

### Svarsalternativ

- ✅ Plato
- ⬜ Socrates
- ⬜ Buddha
- ⬜ Karl Marx
- ⬜ Gandhi

### Ordförklaringar (marginal)

- **cave** — *a big dark hole in a rock or under the ground*
- **prisoner** — *a person who is not free and cannot go out*
- **shadow** — *the dark shape a thing makes when it blocks light*
- **chained** — *tied with metal rings, so you cannot move away*

### Huvudbild

- **Motiv:** Classic illustration of Plato's cave: prisoners chained at the legs, sitting and facing a wall with shadows on it, a fire burning behind them, and one free person walking up toward the bright cave opening with sunlight outside.
- **Bildtext:** *The prisoners see only shadows on the wall.*
- **Shutterstock-sökning:** `Plato allegory of the cave illustration`
- **AI-prompt (alternativ):** Educational illustration of Plato's allegory of the cave: seated prisoners chained so they cannot turn their heads, facing a stone wall with dark shadow figures on it, a fire behind them, one free person walking toward the bright cave exit where sunlight streams in, dramatic but child-friendly, no violence, no text.

### Sidobild

- **Motiv:** A hand making a shadow shape (for example a bird) on a wall in the light of a lamp. Helps explain the word 'shadow' and that a shadow is not the real thing.
- **Bildtext:** *A hand makes a shadow on the wall.*
- **Shutterstock-sökning:** `hand shadow puppet wall light`
- **AI-prompt (alternativ):** Simple photo-style image: a hand in front of a lamp makes a bird-shaped shadow on a plain wall, warm light, dark clear shadow, no text.

## Kort 3 · Årskurs 12 (A) — The perfect circle

**Kategori:** `civilizations`

### Korttext

> Mr X starts a famous school in Athens.  
> He is a student of Socrates.  
> Mr X is a philosopher, not a mathematician.  
> But his school loves geometry.  
> Try this: draw a circle.  
> Look closely: the line is always a little bit wrong.  
> No drawing is ever a perfect circle.  
> But the idea of a circle is perfect.  
> Mr X teaches that our eyes see only imperfect copies.  
> Only the mind can see the perfect ideas.  
> Geometry trains the mind to work with perfect ideas.  
> Ask yourself: do you trust your eyes, or your mind?  
> **Who is Mr X?**

### Svarsalternativ

- ✅ Plato
- ⬜ Pythagoras
- ⬜ Euclid
- ⬜ Socrates
- ⬜ Confucius

### Ordförklaringar (marginal)

- **philosopher** — *a person who thinks hard about big questions*
- **mathematician** — *a person who works with numbers and shapes*
- **geometry** — *mathematics about shapes, like circles and triangles*
- **imperfect** — *not perfect; with small mistakes*

### Huvudbild

- **Motiv:** A young student draws a large circle in the sand with a stick while the philosopher Plato watches and points; a few other students look on; olive trees in the background (his school stands in an olive grove). Shows the school practicing geometry.
- **Bildtext:** *A student draws a circle in the sand.*
- **Shutterstock-sökning:** `ancient greek philosopher students geometry drawing sand illustration`
- **AI-prompt (alternativ):** Illustration: in a sunny olive grove, a young student in a simple robe draws a large circle in the sand with a wooden stick while the bearded ancient Greek philosopher Plato watches and points, a few other students look on, warm daylight, no text.

### Sidobild

- **Motiv:** Two pencil circles side by side: a wobbly freehand circle and a very neat circle drawn with a compass. Production note: do not label either circle as 'perfect' - even the neat compass circle is only a copy; only the idea of the circle is perfect. Helps explain 'imperfect copies'.
- **Bildtext:** *Every circle on paper is a little bit wrong.*
- **Shutterstock-sökning:** `hand drawn circle sketch compass circle pencil comparison`
- **AI-prompt (alternativ):** Clean educational graphic: on the left a wobbly, imperfect freehand pencil circle, on the right a very neat pencil circle drawn with a compass, both clearly pencil drawings on paper, plain light background, no text, no labels.

## Kort 4 · Årskurs 12 (B) — Who should rule?

**Kategori:** `civilizations`

### Korttext

> Mr X lives in Athens.  
> One day, a big jury judges his teacher, Socrates.  
> Hundreds of people vote, and the teacher must die.  
> So Mr X does not trust every decision of the crowd.  
> A clever talker can trick a crowd.  
> Later, Mr X writes a famous book called The Republic.  
> The book asks: what makes a state fair?  
> He says: rulers must be wise and love learning.  
> Not the richest people, not the loudest people.  
> A good ruler loves truth more than power.  
> Mr X teaches this to his student Aristotle.  
> His question is still alive today: how should we choose our leaders?  
> **Who is Mr X?**

### Svarsalternativ

- ✅ Plato
- ⬜ Aristotle
- ⬜ Socrates
- ⬜ Karl Marx
- ⬜ Nelson Mandela

### Ordförklaringar (marginal)

- **jury** — *a group of people who decide if someone did wrong*
- **state** — *a country and the people who lead it*
- **ruler** — *a person who leads a country*

### Huvudbild

- **Motiv:** The ancient Greek philosopher Plato as an older bearded man in a robe, holding a papyrus scroll and pointing upward. Production notes: single figure only - do not use images that also show Aristotle (such as the full Raphael 'School of Athens' fresco), and the name Plato must not be visible anywhere in the image.
- **Bildtext:** *Mr X holds his book about a fair state.*
- **Shutterstock-sökning:** `Plato philosopher statue portrait`
- **AI-prompt (alternativ):** Dignified illustration of the ancient Greek philosopher Plato as an older bearded man in a robe, holding a closed papyrus scroll in one hand and pointing upward with the other, classical marble hall in the background, warm classical colors, single figure, no text.

### Sidobild

- **Motiv:** A crowd of people in an open-air meeting voting by raising their hands. Illustrates voting and the question of how we choose our leaders. Production note: this shows an open assembly vote, not the jury at Socrates' trial - Athenian juries voted with small ballot tokens, not raised hands, so do not link this image to the trial.
- **Bildtext:** *The people vote with raised hands.*
- **Shutterstock-sökning:** `people voting raised hands assembly outdoors`
- **AI-prompt (alternativ):** Simple illustration of a large group of people in an open-air meeting raising their hands to vote, seen from behind, neutral clothing so it fits any culture, clear daylight, no text.

## Kort 5 · Universitet — What is knowledge?

**Kategori:** `civilizations`

### Korttext

> Mr X starts a school in Athens.  
> His teacher is Socrates.  
> The school stays open for about three hundred years.  
> Mr X asks a hard question: what is knowledge?  
> A lucky guess can be true, but it is not knowledge.  
> In one book he tests an answer.  
> You know something when your true belief has good reasons.  
> In another book, a boy with no schooling solves a problem about a square.  
> The boy only answers good questions, step by step.  
> Mr X says: questions wake up the mind, so learning is like remembering.  
> A famous modern thinker says: European philosophy is footnotes to Mr X's books.  
> So when you read the news, ask: do I know this, or do I only believe it?  
> **Who is Mr X?**

### Svarsalternativ

- ✅ Plato
- ⬜ Socrates
- ⬜ Confucius
- ⬜ Buddha
- ⬜ Darwin

### Ordförklaringar (marginal)

- **belief** — *something you think is true*
- **knowledge** — *the things you really know, not only believe*
- **philosophy** — *thinking hard about big questions*
- **footnote** — *a small extra note at the bottom of a page*

### Huvudbild

- **Motiv:** A white marble bust of the ancient Greek philosopher Plato beside a stack of old books on a wooden table, showing him as the thinker later philosophy builds on. Hard requirement: the name Plato must not be visible anywhere in the image - no plinth inscription, no label, no overlaid caption; picture editors must crop or reject labeled busts, because a visible name would give away the quiz answer.
- **Bildtext:** *Mr X asks: what is knowledge?*
- **Shutterstock-sökning:** `Plato marble bust old books`
- **AI-prompt (alternativ):** Elegant photo-style image: a white marble bust of an ancient Greek bearded philosopher beside a stack of old leather books on a wooden table, soft library light, calm and scholarly mood, no text, no letters, no inscription on the bust or its base.

### Sidobild

- **Motiv:** A square drawn in sand with its diagonal line and a larger square built on that diagonal, like the geometry lesson with the boy in Plato's book Meno. A stick points at the drawing. Helps explain how the boy solves the square problem only by answering questions.
- **Bildtext:** *The boy solves the square problem by answering questions.*
- **Shutterstock-sökning:** `square diagonal geometry drawing sand stick`
- **AI-prompt (alternativ):** Simple educational illustration: a square drawn in smooth sand with its diagonal line, and a second larger square drawn on the diagonal, a wooden stick pointing at the lines, top-down view, warm sand colors, no text.
