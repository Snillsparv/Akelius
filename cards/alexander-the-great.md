## Kort 1 · Årskurs 6 — The Boy and the Horse No One Can Ride

**Kategori:** `civilizations`

### Korttext

> King X is a young king from Macedonia, near Greece.  
> A famous old story tells about him as a boy.  
> There is a horse that no one can ride.  
> Everyone is afraid of the horse, but the boy watches it carefully.  
> He sees that the horse is afraid of its own shadow.  
> He turns the horse toward the sun, and the horse becomes calm.  
> He wins the horse with understanding, not with force.  
> The boy's teacher is the famous thinker Aristotle.  
> Later, he leads his army east, all the way to India.  
> In Egypt, he builds a new city and gives it his own name.  
> The name of this city sounds like the name of King X.  
> **Who is King X?**

### Svarsalternativ

- ⬜ Julius Caesar
- ✅ Alexander the Great
- ⬜ Augustus
- ⬜ Hammurabi

### Ordförklaringar (marginal)

- **shadow** — *the dark shape you make on the ground when you stand in the sun*
- **force** — *strong power; when you push or fight to make something happen*
- **understanding** — *when you watch and learn why something happens*

### Huvudbild

- **Motiv:** A teenage boy in simple ancient Greek clothes gently calms a big black horse. He turns the horse's head toward the low sun. Open field, warm light. Production note: no text, letters, or labels anywhere in the image; the boy must stay anonymous.
- **Bildtext:** *The boy turns the horse toward the sun. The horse becomes calm.*
- **Shutterstock-sökning:** `young man calming black horse sunset field`
- **AI-prompt (alternativ):** Warm storybook illustration: a teenage boy in ancient Greek clothing gently holds the head of a large black horse and turns it toward the low sun; open plain, soft golden light; no text, no letters, no modern objects.

### Sidobild

- **Motiv:** A horse standing in bright sunlight with a clear dark shadow on the ground, seen from the side. This image helps explain the word 'shadow'. No text in the image.
- **Bildtext:** *The sun makes a shadow. The horse is afraid of its own shadow.*
- **Shutterstock-sökning:** `horse shadow ground bright sunlight`
- **AI-prompt (alternativ):** Clear simple image: a horse stands in bright sun on flat sandy ground, its dark shadow stretches clearly beside it; no people, no text.

**Kategori:** `civilizations`

### Korttext

> King X is a young king from the Greek world.  
> His armies go east, all the way to India.

> The oldest history books we still have are written centuries after his death.  
> So historians must ask which stories are facts and which are dreams.
